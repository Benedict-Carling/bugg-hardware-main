# Bugg r6 main board (unofficial, unbuilt)

An experimental revision of the Bugg r5 main board by Benedict Carling (Alauda). **It is not an official Bugg release, and no r6 board has been built or measured.**

It is derived from `r5/src` at commit `e71fd5740865aec2923b8e5c67f93d5d7822d4b3` of [bugg-resources/bugg-hardware-main](https://github.com/bugg-resources/bugg-hardware-main), and is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 like the original, with credit to Bugg's original hardware authors.

## How it was made

The schematic changes, placement and routing were produced with AI coding agents driving KiCad through scripts, and reviewed by me. The review documents in `review/` are AI-assisted review passes. The design has not been reviewed by an independent electronics engineer.

## What changed

- **Plug-in microphone bias filter.** R104 goes from 0 Ω to 100 Ω with the existing 10 µF C83: calculated 34 dB supply-ripple rejection at 8 kHz, or 26 dB if C83 derates to 4 µF. Q25 (the P3V3 mic-power switch) now takes its supply from before R104, so that output keeps its full voltage.
- **ADC input filter.** C75/C76 go from 180 pF to 4.7 nF C0G: calculated 18 dB more rejection at 1.38 MHz and 0.06 dB loss at 20 kHz. This is an experimental 44.1/48 kHz population; 180 pF remains the alternative.
- **Real-time clock.** The DS3231M becomes an RV-3032-C7: ±2.5 ppm instead of ±5 ppm initial specification over −40 to +85 °C, excluding aging.
- **Wake buffer.** U14 becomes an SN74LVC1G37 in the same footprint, with a Schmitt input suited to the slow RC wake edge. R29/R30/C36 become 100 kΩ / 47 kΩ / 1 µF.
- **3.3 V always-on rail.** A TPS7A02 regulator powers the clock, the wake logic and the voltage monitor, which r5 ran from the approximately 5.4 V system rail against 5.5 V part limits.
- **Supply voltage monitoring.** An ADS1115 measures the input, system, modem and USB rails. It measures voltage only, not current or battery charge.
- **Modem shutdown confirmation.** VGPIO and SAFE_PWR_REMOVE are level-shifted and latched, then read over I²C. This addresses r5 erratum 5.

The full part list is in [review/BOM-CHANGES.md](review/BOM-CHANGES.md): 8 changed, 33 added.

## What did not change

- **Board:** the outline, the six-layer stack and the 1.6 mm thickness.
- **Fixed interfaces:** all 37 keep their position, rotation, side and pad geometry. That covers the CM4, the modem, the USB, J10 and FH12 connectors, both U.FL connectors, the SIM, microSD and coin-cell holders, the mounting holes, bosses, ground contacts, J1–J9 and the fiducials.
- **Copper and test points:** the USB and microphone-input traces are identical to r5, and all 59 r5 test points remain.

## Caveats

- **Nothing is measured.** All figures are calculations or datasheet specifications. There is no claim of lower recorded self-noise, longer battery life or better reliability.
- **KiCad 10 is required.** The files were saved in KiCad 10 format, so the diff against r5 includes format changes.
- **Some sheets were regenerated by script.** The RTC sheet and the two new sheets were written by script rather than edited by hand, so the schematic diffs are larger than the actual changes.
- **Not a drop-in r5 image.** The new RTC and monitoring need Bugg OS integration, and `firmware/` contains support code with 22 unit tests against simulated hardware.
- **Still to validate on a prototype:** ADC settling and distortion, microphone noise, wake timing, modem shutdown timing, power sequencing and enclosure fit.

## Reproducing the checks

Run these from `r6/`:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `<KiCad 10 python> design/verify_board.py` | Compares the board against `r5/src` and checks schematic/PCB pad nets |
| `python3 design/calculations.py` | Recomputes the filter and clock figures from the netlist |
| `python3 design/bom.py` | Regenerates the BOM delta |
| `cd firmware && python3 -m unittest discover -p 'test_*.py'` | Runs the firmware tests |

The native KiCad results are recorded in `review/final-drc.json` and `review/erc.json`: 0 DRC errors, 0 unconnected items, 0 schematic-parity issues and 0 ERC errors.

Start with [review/REPORT.md](review/REPORT.md). [review/AUDIO-EXPLAINED.md](review/AUDIO-EXPLAINED.md) explains what the filter figures mean for recordings.
